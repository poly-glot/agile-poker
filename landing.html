The concept

Planning poker, simplified

Planning poker is a consensus-based technique for estimating effort. Each team member selects a card representing their estimate, and all cards are revealed simultaneously — avoiding anchoring bias and encouraging honest assessment.

  1. Create a session

    One click, no setup. You get a unique session URL instantly.

  2. Share the link

    Send the URL to your team. They join without signing up.

  3. Vote together

    Everyone picks their estimation card privately, in real-time.

  4. Reveal and discuss

    Cards flip simultaneously. Discuss outliers, reach consensus.
